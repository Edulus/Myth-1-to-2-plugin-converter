Q7. Feature and bug reports for the web converter

In short (as of 8 November 2026) This is the place to report problems with the converter that runs in your browser. Keep asked for a version number, a list of changes and a "please test next" list on the converter page, and it now has all three. His reports led to buildings appearing in version 0.6, to a crash at the edge of Crow's Bridge being fixed in 0.7, and to the ground meeting the walls of Crow's Bridge's bridge in 0.8. The crash fix works for every map, not just Crow's Bridge. With a screenshot, Keep showed that the ground does follow the bridge now, but the game doesn't draw the bridge's deck, so units seem to walk on air. We are testing the cause in the game. On Tandem he now means three single-storey farmhouses, and we have asked him for a picture. Units still moonwalk.

Please enter any feature or bug reports here about the webtool for converting TFL to M2 plugins.

This question is only for the web converter. It helps to include the converter's version number (shown at the top of its page), the plugin you converted, your browser, and what happened. For a problem in a converted plugin, also paste the start of the Technical details shown after converting.
