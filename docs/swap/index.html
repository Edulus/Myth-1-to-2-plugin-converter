Swap the Civil War soldiers for regular units

Play the Civil War maps for Myth II with regular Myth II units. Choose which ordinary units, such as warriors or ghols, stand in place of the Civil War soldiers, and this page writes a new plugin. It runs entirely in this browser tab: your files are not uploaded anywhere, the new plugin is written on your own computer, and your original files are never changed.

Thank you, Obelix

This page exists because Obelix asked for it: a way to play the Civil War maps with regular Myth II units, with the player choosing which unit replaces which. A request like that is how this project grows, and we are grateful for it.

Good to know

  • Beta. The swapping itself was checked by hand on a clean Myth II 1.8.5 with Civil War Reloaded 1.8.4 and the Fields of Battle map pack: real warriors and ghols appeared on Fort Sumter and Little Flat Top. Other maps, other units and other Civil War packs are untried, and this browser version is new. Tell us how it goes.
  • Use a computer, not a phone, with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The files are read in place rather than loaded whole, but the new plugin is about as big as the maps plugin (around 520 MB for Civil War Reloaded 1.8.4), so keep some disk space and close other tabs.
  • To play: in Myth II choose the new campaign first, then activate the tagset under Tagsets. The new plugin has to load after the tagset. The campaign list unlocks levels in order, so only the first ones are playable at first.
  • You supply the Civil War files. None of them, and no Myth II game files, are included or copied to this site.
  • The swap was first checked against Civil War Reloaded 1.8.4 by PoinT of ARMY Munitions Factory. That plugin builds on work by IronDuke (Project Magma), Cydonian, Thoran and others credited in its own readme. It is a third-party plugin.
  • How did it go? Tell us in Q9: Tried converting a map of your own?, report problems in Q7, or leave a comment below.
