Updated 19 November 2026: this page offers converter 0.19. The list below says what changed in each version; what Keep has confirmed in the game is on the home page.

Version 0.19 · built 19 November 2026

Please test next

  • Dwarf Heroes: can they throw uphill now, without walking up to the spot first? Downhill and on flat ground still fine?
  • Dwarven Pathfinders (if a level has them): the same question for their bottle throw.
  • Forest Giants and Old Growth Forest Giants: when one dies and its pieces are blown up, are the chunks teal like the Yearlings'? (We expect yes.)
  • If you can still run The Fallen Lords itself: what colour are those chunks there?

Report what you find in Q7: feature and bug reports, with the version number.

What changed

  • 0.19 (19 November 2026): A likely fix for Dwarf Heroes that would not throw uphill. In The Fallen Lords the hero's bottle throw has a throwing speed of 92 with a random spread of minus 81, and the converter copied that. Myth II has no negative spread in any of Bungie's or Magma's attacks, and Magma's own port of this throw has none, so it is now 0: the hero always throws at 92 (an ordinary dwarf throws at 66 to 74). The Dwarven Pathfinder's bottle throw had the same setting and gets the same fix. The teal Yearling pieces are not fixed yet: the chunks are trow pieces (the Forest Giants' pieces break into them when hit) drawn without the trow's colouring, and we need one more test in the game before choosing a fix.
  • 0.18 (17 November 2026): Heals should work now. Myth II refuses to let an ability be used on friendly units unless it says so, and The Fallen Lords never had that setting, so converted Journeymen and Heron Guards could heal neither other units nor themselves. A special ability that fires a healing projectile now carries the setting, as it does in Bungie's own Journeymen and Heron Guards. This also covers the Yearlings' mandrake heal, which is less certain.
  • 0.17 (17 November 2026): Two bugs found that may be why Forest Giants and Trow never attacked. First, every unit was marked giant-sized in Myth II (the converter read The Fallen Lords' AI-intelligence setting as the size); now only the Forest Giant and the trow are, as in Bungie's and Magma's versions, and everyone else is man-sized. Second, each attack's flags were copied raw, which scrambled the bits that say whether an attack is for giants or not for giants; they are now translated, so the Forest Giant's and trow's attacks come out exactly as in Bungie's own tags.
  • 0.16 (16 November 2026): A likely fix for units jogging on the spot after they stop. The Fallen Lords leaves each unit's pause and turning animations empty, and Myth II plays the pause animation when a unit stops. Bungie's own Myth II versions of the same units fill them, with the unit's idle and moving animations; the converter now does the same. Also new, because Keep asked for it: after converting, you can save a change list, every setting the converter wrote in each unit with its old value, its new value and why, to check the converter's work.
  • 0.15 (16 November 2026): Each unit now gets the pathfinding room Project Magma's port gives it, instead of one value for every unit. The Forest Giant gets 220 and the trow 154, where both had 77, which may be why they would not attack. Of the 17 monster settings Myth II has and The Fallen Lords does not, these are the only two that differ between units in Magma's port.
  • 0.14 (16 November 2026): A likely fix for the black or streaky ground after explosions. Keep sent the same converted map before and after saving it in Loathing, and the main thing Loathing rewrites is the map's level-of-detail data. The converter wrote it as zeros. No map made by Bungie or Project Magma has a single zero there, so the converter now writes what Loathing writes.
  • 0.13 (16 November 2026): Units are now drawn at Myth II's size. They were about a third too large, so thrall stood out of the water. Myth II's own files hold the thrall, dwarf, ghol, soulless and spider of The Fallen Lords with the same pictures, and Bungie drew each at 0.73 of its old size; converted units now get the same. Ambient sounds are also converted, and one damaged part of a plugin no longer stops the whole conversion.
  • 0.12 (16 November 2026): Free-for-all and team maps now use Myth II's own flags and balls. The copies the converter made were not recognised by the game, so a hosted game still showed no flags or balls even after 0.11 made the units and scenery appear.
  • 0.11 (15 November 2026): Free-for-all and team maps now tell Myth II which game types their units, scenery, flags and balls belong to. Before, every one was marked as appearing in no game, so a hosted game started with no teams, flags or balls and ended at once. Keep found the cause.
  • 0.10 (15 November 2026): Converted maps now say they are Myth II maps. They used to keep The Fallen Lords' version number, which made Myth II's editor Oak refuse them and may have made the game treat them as older maps.
  • 0.9 (9 November 2026): The deck of Crow's Bridge's bridge is drawn again, so units crossing it no longer seem to walk on air, and the arch is dark underneath as in The Fallen Levels v2. The ground's markings are now translated to Myth II's meanings instead of copied from The Fallen Lords, on every map.
  • 0.8 (2 November 2026): The ground beside bridges and other buildings no longer hides their walls. On Crow's Bridge it now meets the bridge's stone sides instead of sagging away below the railings.
  • 0.7 (30 October 2026): Fixed a crash when scrolling to the edge of some maps, such as Crow's Bridge and crazyglue's Along the way to... The border at each map edge is also as wide as in The Fallen Lords again.
  • 0.6 (27 October 2026): Buildings and other 3D models now appear, such as houses, barns, inns and bridges, and units walk around them.
  • 0.5 (27 October 2026): Plants, bushes and units sit on the ground instead of floating above it or sinking into it.
  • 0.4 (27 October 2026): Fixed missing unit sounds that stopped some levels from loading, such as On the road to Strand in Sir Mauriac's Tale.
  • 0.3 (27 October 2026): Hills and slopes are smooth, not stepped.
  • 0.2 (27 October 2026): Water now acts as water: units wade and slow down instead of walking on it.
  • 0.1 (26 October 2026): First beta: convert a plugin in your browser.

Turn a Myth: The Fallen Lords plugin into one that Myth II: Soulblighter 1.8.5 can play. It runs entirely in this browser tab: your file is not uploaded anywhere. Only the plugin's name, its number of levels, the converter version and the date are added to the public list further down, and only while the box below stays ticked. Untick it to keep your conversion off the list.

Good to know

  • Use a computer, not a phone. Converting needs a lot of memory; close other tabs if it fails.
  • The first time, the page fetches the original Fallen Lords game files (320 MB) from this website. Your browser keeps them for next time.
  • The converted plugin carries the original game files it needs, so you don't need anything else installed.
  • Beta. Tested with Sir Mauriac's Tale on Windows. Other plugins may convert with parts missing, and the report tells you what it could not convert.
  • Only share a converted plugin if its author agrees.
  • After you try a converted plugin, please tell us how it went in Q9: Tried converting a map of your own?, even if it worked.
  • Found a bug, or want a feature? Tell us in Q7: feature and bug reports.

An unofficial, non-commercial fan project for the 1990s games Myth: The Fallen Lords and Myth II, not affiliated with Bungie. It never asks for an email address, a password or a payment. Its downloads are Myth plugin data files for the game to read, not programs to install or run.
