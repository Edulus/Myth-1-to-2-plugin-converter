Please test next

  • Bridges: cross every bridge with a few units. Is the deck drawn under their feet? Name any bridge where they seem to walk on air.
  • Water and banks: walk units along rivers, into shallow water and up to deep water. Name any place where they now cross water they shouldn't, or are stopped where they could walk before.
  • Ground: name any ground that is missing, see-through or drawn oddly.
  • Levels: does every level start? Name any that won't load.

Report what you find in Q7: feature and bug reports, with the version number.

What changed

  • 0.9 (9 November 2026): The deck of Crow's Bridge's bridge is drawn again, so units crossing it no longer seem to walk on air, and the arch is dark underneath as in The Fallen Levels v2. The ground's markings are now translated to Myth II's meanings instead of copied from The Fallen Lords, on every map.
  • 0.8 (2 November 2026): The ground beside bridges and other buildings no longer hides their walls. On Crow's Bridge it now meets the bridge's stone sides instead of sagging away below the railings.
  • 0.7 (30 October 2026): Fixed a crash when scrolling to the edge of some maps, such as Crow's Bridge and crazyglue's Along the way to... The border at each map edge is also as wide as in The Fallen Lords again.
  • 0.6 (27 October 2026): Buildings and other 3D models now appear, such as houses, barns, inns and bridges, and units walk around them.
  • 0.5 (27 October 2026): Plants, bushes and units sit on the ground instead of floating above it or sinking into it.
  • 0.4 (27 October 2026): Fixed missing unit sounds that stopped some levels from loading, such as On the road to Strand in Sir Mauriac's Tale.
  • 0.3 (27 October 2026): Hills and slopes are smooth, not stepped.
  • 0.2 (27 October 2026): Water now acts as water: units wade and slow down instead of walking on it.
  • 0.1 (26 October 2026): First beta: convert a plugin in your browser.

Turn a Myth: The Fallen Lords plugin into one that Myth II: Soulblighter 1.8.5 can play. It runs entirely in this browser tab: your file is not uploaded anywhere. Only the plugin's name, its number of levels, the converter version and the date are added to the public list further down, and only while the box below stays ticked. Untick it to keep your conversion off the list.

Good to know

  • Use a computer, not a phone. Converting needs a lot of memory; close other tabs if it fails.
  • The first time, the page fetches the original Fallen Lords game files (320 MB) from this website. Your browser keeps them for next time.
  • The converted plugin carries the original game files it needs, so you don't need anything else installed.
  • Beta. Tested with Sir Mauriac's Tale on Windows. Other plugins may convert with parts missing, and the report tells you what it could not convert.
  • Only share a converted plugin if its author agrees.
  • After you try a converted plugin, please tell us how it went in Q9: Tried converting a map of your own?, even if it worked.
  • Found a bug, or want a feature? Tell us in Q7: feature and bug reports.
