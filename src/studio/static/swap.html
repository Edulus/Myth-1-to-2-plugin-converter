Swap the units in a plugin's maps

Built for the Civil War plugins: pick which regular units appear in place of the Civil War soldiers. A new plugin is written; the original is never changed.

You need the plugin that holds the maps, the tagset its units live in, and your Myth II folder (the original units are read from it, and nothing is copied anywhere else).
